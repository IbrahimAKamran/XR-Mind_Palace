# 3-minute demo script

**Setup before judges arrive**
- Chrome, `npm run dev`, mic + camera allowed.
- Palace → *Load demo people* (Maya + Jordan exist).
- One teammate (**A**) has already been enrolled through a real encounter.
- Panel open on the Encounter tab, standing in the lobby.

**0:00 · Hook**
"You meet 50 people at a hackathon. By Sunday you remember three. We built a memory palace where every person becomes a room, and the room is generated from their voice."

**0:20 · Walk the palace**
WASD through Maya's door. Point out: her objects (job, hobby, dog), the timeline wall of past chats (click a card), the colours and wallpaper coming from her voiceprint.

**0:50 · Meet a judge live**
Exit to the lobby → *Start encounter*. Ask the judge to introduce themselves.
→ New voice → read the consent line → judge says **"yes"** (verbal consent is detected).
→ Fill *Where* / *How we met* → *Start conversation*.
→ Ask what they work on and what they do for fun. **Objects pop into their brand-new room as they talk.**
→ *End conversation*: the card lands on the timeline wall.

**1:50 · Recognition**
Teammate **A** clicks *Start encounter* and talks → "This sounds like A (87%)" → their room loads instantly.

**2:15 · Make it yours + AR**
Drag an object, `C` to recolour, scroll to raise it. Toggle **Passthrough**: the room turns to glass over the real world. Mention it runs as WebXR on Quest with controllers.

**2:40 · Privacy close**
"Consent before anything is saved, no audio stored, everything on-device, one-click revoke." Show *Revoke consent & delete*.
